## THE ARCHITECTURAL INVERSION
A 45-term quadratic over 8 features was **replacing** geometry once 6 samples existed. Fitted to a
cluster, it extrapolated violently: the dot flew off with the smallest movement.

Measured, trained on a clustered set and tested across the whole field:

| samples | polynomial replaces geometry | **geometry + residual** |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | median 383 px, **p95 999 px** | median **122**, p95 **319** |
| 15 | 349, **1037** | **172**, **352** |
| 35 | 212, 660 | 202, **399** |

A p95 of 999 px on a 1080-tall display is off-screen. **Geometry is now the backbone**, learning a
bounded residual, and `Calibrator.fit` k-folds the residual against geometry-alone and **discards
it unless it wins**. On the real 17 samples it discards (pure geometry, 27 px); in the 240-sample
sim it keeps it (11.8 px).

Holloway (Presence 6, 1997): system delay is the **largest single registration error**, because
every other term is constant while latency scales with **head angular velocity**.

|---|---|---|
| capture + process (17.9 fps) | 56 ms | 56 ms |

## THE XREAL IMU: decoded from scratch
**Not HID** (those interfaces open and produce nothing). The glasses are a **USB network device**:
`en8`, host 169.254.2.10, **glasses at 169.254.2.1, TCP 52998**, streaming on connect.

```
134-byte records, magic 28 36 00 00, ~1100-1400 Hz
offset 0x22  3x float32 LE  GYRO   rad/s
offset 0x2e  3x float32 LE  ACCEL  m/s^2
```
**Confirmed by physics:** stationary |accel| = **9.773 m/s²** vs gravity 9.81, 0.38% off. A wrong
offset would still decode to finite floats, which is why `--live` re-checks it.

**Axis map measured** (`--map`): `du←wy(yaw) +`, `dv←wx(pitch) −`, |corr| **0.99**, fitted/theoretical
0.97, independently confirming `WORLD_FOV=70`. Three fixes got it from 0.13 to 0.99: **optical flow
not the dot**, **integrate not differentiate**, **constrain the fit to the physics** (a free 2×3 fit
attributed u to ROLL with a coefficient of −2.76 where the whole plausible range is 0.82).

## LIGHTING: solved in software, no hardware
Corpus spanned brightness **139–161**. Live eyeR sat at **49**, entirely outside it.

| | normal | dark ×0.5 | dark ×0.34 |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | 25 px | 206 | 715 |
| mean-normalised | 26 | 25 | 23 |
| **median-normalised** | **21** | **21** | **21** |

Median beats mean because a blown-out corner drags the mean up (eyeL: mean 39, **median 11**).

Then **gradient augmentation** at retrain, ramps 0.15–0.75, gamma 0.6–1.4, gain 0.30–1.50:

| model | clean | dark | **side-lit** |
|---|---|---|---|
| old | 24 px | 25 | **269** |
| new | 34 | 29 | **76** |

3.5× better where it was catastrophic, 42% worse where it was fine. **Illumination flattening was
tested and REJECTED**, fixes gradients but destroys clean (26→243 px), because the model never saw
flattened images.

## DISPLAY SCALE: the constant that had been guessed
Two methods: wall (46.71°) and fitting to the 17 ground-truth samples (**48.25°**, 27 px vs 32 px at
the old 50.0). They do **not** fully reconcile, D was measured from the cam lens, 50.5 mm forward of
the eye, which pushes the physical value *down* to ~45.2°.

**Resolution: `DISPLAY_FOV_DEG` is an EFFECTIVE SCALE, not the physical FOV.** It is the only free
scale between direction and pixel, so it absorbs unmodelled distortion (k1/k2 still placeholders),
the eye-position assumption, and residual world-camera error. **Do not "fix" it to the physical
value**, that is more faithful and measurably worse (43 px vs 27 px).

## THE EYE-CORNER FEATURES: wired in, inert, and why
`geometric_pixel` had been reading only `x[0:4]` and **discarding the eye features entirely**. The
whole canthus pipeline was gating frame usability and contributing nothing to where the pixel goes.
Now implemented (`eye_offset_mm`), guarded, and **`EYE_SHIFT_GAIN = 0.0`**.

Swept against the real samples: gain 0.00 → **26.9 px** (best), 0.50 → 27.3, 1.00 → 27.6.
**The reason is in the data:** eye-feature spread was sd **0.032 / 0.0085**, the glasses barely
moved. A glasses-position correction cannot help when the glasses did not move.
**→ RE-SEAT THE GLASSES between batches next run, then re-run the sweep.**

## THINGS I GOT WRONG (kept so they are not repeated)
- Claimed saturation detects worn-vs-desk. It does not, a dim room read 0.2% and passed.
- Claimed flat-skin separates worn from room. Classes **overlap** (worn 83–92, room 77–83); it
  false-negatived a rig locking 88%. Now an advisory corroborated by lock state.
- Claimed `cameras.py`'s 4:3 assumption "measured not to matter", I had tested a software resize,
  not the camera.
- Inferred an eye-camera swap from OFF-FACE data and had to withdraw it.
- Switched the display to Extended with the mirror master/follower backwards.
- Re-aimed at a target that was already correctly in frame, at 1–3 m it is ~30 px wide,
  and a full-frame glance cannot settle presence. **Zoom before doubting the setup.**
- Three separate selftests measured nothing until rewritten (a ramp that clipped; a steady ramp
  where a fixed EMA looks fine; nominal values taken from the wrong source).
